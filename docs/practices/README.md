## Parent principle

**Build and maintain justified confidence through proportional evidence.**

Investigate relevant knowledge, challenge material assumptions, check outcomes using evidence
suited to the actual claim, and preserve the understanding needed for future judgment. The goal
is confidence appropriate to consequences, uncertainty and context, not universal proof or
certainty before action. Justified confidence reflects available evidence and its limitations;
subjective conviction, consensus or authorization alone does not establish factual truth.
Proportionality changes the amount and form of effort, not the obligation to be honest about
evidence and limitations. Make remaining uncertainty visible when material.

## Governance and applicability

[ADR 0004's authority and applicability decision](../adr/0004-transversal-practices-and-learning.md#1-authority-applicability-and-justified-deviations)
governs these practices: relevance determines presumed applicability without requiring project
opt-in; genuine inapplicability needs no exception. Material departures require explicit,
context-specific and sufficiently strong justification proportional to the importance and
consequences involved. Projects retain authority over their domain decisions.

ADR 0004 is the authoritative treatment of applicability, deviations, insufficient evidence and
authority conflicts; this index provides navigation and a brief summary. Guidance shapes
authorized work; it does not itself authorize new work or waive stronger constraints.

[Learning and promotion](../adr/0004-transversal-practices-and-learning.md#2-learning-critical-evaluation-and-promotion)
and the [minimum feedback flows](../adr/0004-transversal-practices-and-learning.md#5-minimum-conceptual-feedback-flows)
govern evolution. Capture is not promotion; exceptions can inform deliberate revision. The index
and practice definitions should reflect accepted changes while decision history preserves prior
meaning. Their editorial structure is not a required schema for future practices.
